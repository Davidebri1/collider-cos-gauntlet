# CROWD / PEER JURY — Cheat Sheet

**Who:** the competitors themselves. The organizer referees; the jury decides.

## When you vote
1. **Round-opening feature vote** — which feature everyone builds; declare copy/challenge intent. Anonymous. Highest votes win.
2. **Head-to-head votes** — after judging sorts entries into Superior/Qualified/Unqualified, Superior entries are paired randomly. You vote the winner with reasons. Popular vote wins.

## Rules of the jury (§7, §9)
- **Reasons are mandatory.** "I just liked it" is a baseless vote.
- Your votes and feedback are evaluated like evidence in court. Baseless conjecture, illogical claims, misplaced requirements or aims are flagged on discovery and **cost against your final score**.
- **No ganging up on the front-runner.** The majority of every score is demonstration of understanding — coordbehind against a leader fails on its own merits, and your vote quality is scored regardless.
- A strong, fully developed logical chain is the winning strategy. You do not need to win rounds to win the gauntlet.

## Anonymity
- Nobody sees who voted for whom.
- Nobody sees who voted copy vs challenge.
- Winners and results are public; voters never are.
